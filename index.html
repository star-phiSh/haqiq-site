The platform

Everything needed to run compliance training without the clutter.

Built around the core jobs an administrator and learner need to complete.

01

Create

Build structured courses with lessons, media, supporting documents, and clear learning objectives.

02

Assign

Assign training to employees and organize users by role, department, and status.

03

Assess

Use final assessments, passing scores, attempt limits, and completion rules to verify understanding.

04

Certify

Issue traceable certificates after successful completion and keep credential status visible.

05

Track

See progress, attempts, completions, course activity, and learning records from one admin view.

06

Report

Turn training activity into clear compliance evidence for internal review and operational follow-up.

How it works

From assignment to evidence in four steps.

1

Publish training

Create a course, lessons, and the final assessment.

2

Assign employees

Give the right people access to the required training.

3

Complete & assess

Learners work through content and complete the required assessment.

4

Record & certify

Haqiq keeps the completion record and issues eligible certificates.
