Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Haqiq is a learning and compliance platform designed to help organizations provide training, assessments, completion records, and certificates to their employees and users.

Information We Process

Depending on how your organization uses Haqiq, we may process information including your name, email address, employee profile information, organization or department information, course assignments, learning progress, assessment attempts and scores, completion records, certificates, and application activity.

Learning Activity

Haqiq may record course activity such as lesson progress, completion status, assessment activity, and active learning time. This information is used to provide training records and compliance reporting to the organization that assigned the training.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and operate the Haqiq service, authenticate users, deliver assigned training, record learning progress, administer assessments, issue certificates, provide compliance reporting, maintain security, and support users.

Organizational Accounts

Haqiq accounts may be created and administered by an employer or other organization. In these cases, the organization may have access to training records associated with users under its account.

Service Providers

Haqiq uses service providers to operate parts of the application, including cloud database, authentication, storage, and related infrastructure services. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their respective services.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Data Retention

Training and compliance records may be retained for periods required by the organization using Haqiq or as necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal or compliance requirements.

Your Rights and Requests

Users may contact their organization administrator or Haqiq support regarding access, correction, deletion, or other requests concerning their personal information, subject to applicable legal and compliance retention requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page together with the revised date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact support@haqiq.app.
