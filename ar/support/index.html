Support

Need help using Haqiq? We can assist with account access, assigned courses, assessments, certificates, and other application questions.

Account Access

If you are unable to access your account, first use the password recovery option available from the Haqiq sign-in screen.

If your account is managed by your employer or organization, contact your organization's Haqiq administrator for assistance.

Technical Support

For technical support, contact support@haqiq.app.

Certificates

Certificates are issued after successful completion of eligible courses and assessments. Certificate availability may depend on the settings configured by your organization.
